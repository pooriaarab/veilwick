# VeilWick brand system

This file defines VeilWick's current brand. Product source remains the proof for
every claim. Do not use the generic template identity in public VeilWick work.

## Identity

VeilWick is a private, adults-only video experience for synthetic wellness and
entertainment. It combines a vertical feed, live rooms, and short series.

**Position:** Private, synthetic entertainment for adults.

**Core message:** Spicy wellness, synthetic and private.

**Proof points:**

- The primary experience is a full-height, vertical video feed.
- Live rooms pair video with lightweight chat.
- Series group short portrait episodes into playlists.
- Synthetic catalogue characters are fictional adults aged 18 or older.
- Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, KV, and Queues form the intended platform.

Do not claim a production launch, real livestreaming, or generated media when a
surface still uses mock data. Name a demo, sample, or placeholder when present.

## Name and wordmark

Write the name as **VeilWick**. Preserve the capital `V` and `W`. Do not write
Veilwick, Veil Wick, VEILWICK, or VeilWick AI as the product name.

The current interface wordmark renders `Veil`, an amber `W`, then `ick`. Use a
heavy Geist Sans weight with tight tracking. Keep the name on one line.

The current compact mark is a white `V` inside an amber-to-rose square. It is a
CSS treatment, not an approved downloadable logo asset. Do not invent a vector
logo, icon set, or clear-space measurement from this treatment.

Use the complete wordmark when space permits. Use the compact mark only inside
the current interface. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or recolor it.

## Voice

Write with calm confidence. Be direct about the experience, the technology,
and the product's current state. Keep legal and safety language unambiguous.

Prefer:

- “Browse the vertical feed.”
- “Synthetic content. Fictional adults 18+.”
- “This demo uses sample media.”
- “Open a live room.”

Avoid:

- Claims that a demo is a live production service.
- Claims that sample media is generated by VeilWick.
- Euphemisms that weaken age or synthetic-content notices.
- Empty claims such as revolutionary, world-class, or limitless.
- Graphic copy in general navigation, onboarding, or brand material.

Use “synthetic” for generated people and scenes. Use “AI-generated” when the
technology matters. Do not use these terms to describe real people or samples.

## Safety and trust

Every adult surface states 18+ access. Synthetic surfaces identify fictional
characters and say no real person appears in synthetic scenes. Recorded-person
surfaces use approved 2257 language.

Keep the age gate blocking until the visitor confirms eligibility. Keep legal
routes available without the blocking gate. Never hide a safety statement in
low-contrast text or behind an interaction.

Do not imply consent, identity, or age from visual appearance. Product data and
approved records must support such claims. Never use a real person's likeness
without documented rights and explicit product approval.

## Color

VeilWick has two coordinated surface modes.

The marketing surface uses cool, near-white neutrals with near-black type. The
media surface uses black or zinc-950 with white overlays. Amber connects both.

| Role            | Tracked value or utility   | Use                                 |
| --------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Light canvas    | `oklch(0.99 0.002 250)`    | Marketing background                |
| Light ink       | `oklch(0.145 0.01 250)`    | Marketing text                      |
| Light line      | `oklch(0.922 0.005 250)`   | Borders and input edges             |
| Media canvas    | `black`, `zinc-950`        | Feed, live, and series              |
| Media text      | `white` with opacity steps | Copy above media                    |
| Brand amber     | `amber-400`, `amber-500`   | The `W`, focus, and key detail      |
| Brand rose      | `rose-500`, `rose-600`     | Compact mark, likes, and live state |
| Premium violet  | `violet-600`               | Premium actions only                |
| Premium fuchsia | `fuchsia-600`              | Premium actions and locked state    |

Do not turn safety states into brand accents. Use semantic success, warning,
error, and information tokens for their stated purpose.

## Typography

Use Geist Sans for interface copy, headings, controls, and the wordmark. Use
Geist Mono for code, routes, identifiers, and numeric technical data.

Use heavy weights for the wordmark and compact media headings. Use bold or
semibold weights for actions and labels. Use regular text for descriptions.

Headings use balanced wrapping. Paragraphs use pretty wrapping. Tables and
explicitly marked numeric displays use tabular numbers. Apply `tabular-nums` to
durations, prices, counts, and timelines.

## Imagery and media

Portrait video is the main visual language. Use a 9:16 crop for feed clips and
series posters. Keep the subject legible behind interface overlays.

Use dark bottom gradients to protect captions and actions. Use restrained top
gradients for navigation. Do not cover a face or required safety statement.

Label placeholders and samples. Do not present third-party sample footage as a
VeilWick generation. Do not publish watermarked, distorted, duplicate, or
unlicensed media.

Keep generated characters fictional. Avoid real-person names, copied likenesses,
or claims that suggest a real performer appears in synthetic content.

## Interface character

The feed feels immersive and compact. The landing surface feels quiet and
technical. Shared type, amber detail, concise copy, and clear states join them.

Use pills for categories, compact status, and playlist state. Use full-height
media for the primary feed. Use cards for marketing facts and poster grids.

Motion supports state changes. It never blocks reading. Always respect reduced
motion. Keyboard focus remains visible on every control.

## Domain and public routes

No VeilWick production domain currently resolves. References to `veilwick.com`
and `cdn.veilwick.com` describe intended infrastructure, not a live service.

When a custom domain launches, `/brand` must show this canonical system.
`/design.md` must return `.agents/design.md` byte for byte as Markdown.

Do not publish either route on an unrelated template domain. Verify DNS,
Cloudflare routing, response status, content type, and exact file bytes.

## Usage rules

- Lead with the feed, live rooms, or series experience.
- Keep VeilWick's name and amber `W` treatment consistent.
- State demo and placeholder status where visitors can see it.
- Keep adult, fictional, and synthetic notices together.
- Use amber for identity and focus, not as a page-wide wash.
- Reserve violet-to-fuchsia gradients for premium states.
- Use rose 500 or 600 for the compact mark, live state, and social feedback.
- Do not expose the generic master-template identity.
- Do not invent a finalized logo asset or production domain.
- Do not let decoration reduce media controls or text contrast.
